What to Look For Before You Buy
Most AI sales tools overpromise on the demo and underdeliver in the workflow. Before you evaluate any tool, apply these four filters:
Does it reduce time-to-follow-up? Speed matters more than personalization in sales. The rep who follows up in 5 minutes wins the deal over the rep who sends a perfect email in 48 hours.
Does it integrate with your existing CRM? A standalone AI tool that requires reps to log into a separate platform will be abandoned within 30 days. The AI needs to live where your reps already work.
Can you measure the impact on pipeline velocity? If you can't tie the tool to deals moved, deals closed, or time saved per rep per week, you're buying a feature, not a result.
Does the pricing make sense at your deal size? A $1,500/month tool is easy to justify when your average deal is $50K. It's impossible to justify when you're closing $3K deals.
