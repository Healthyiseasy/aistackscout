What to Look For Before You Buy
Not every AI writing tool is built for how small businesses actually work. Before you compare features, run every option through these four filters:
First-draft quality that reduces editing time, not just generates words. A tool that produces 1,000 words of generic filler still costs you an hour of editing. The right tool produces drafts your team can publish with 15 minutes of cleanup.
Brand voice consistency without constant babysitting. If every output sounds different, you're trading writing time for editing time. Look for tools that learn your tone and maintain it across users.
Adoption speed for non-writers. Your ops manager, sales reps, and HR lead all write. If the tool requires prompt engineering skills to produce decent output, only your marketing person will use it — and that's not enough ROI.
Pricing that makes sense at your team size. Per-seat pricing punishes growing teams. Look for plans with unlimited users or flat-rate tiers that cover your actual usage.
