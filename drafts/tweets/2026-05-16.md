## Tweet 2 (12pm MT) - Mistake Pattern
The three AI tool mistakes lean teams keep making in 2026:

Buying in the wrong category first. Writing tools are the most visible, so they get bought first. But writing is rarely where operational drag lives. The real drag is in scheduling coordination, meeting follow-through, and research time. Writing tools look busy. They rarely move the needle on hours recovered.

Skipping the replacement question. The teams that get ROI ask "what does this replace?" before they buy. The teams that churn through subscriptions ask "what does this do?" That is a fundamentally different evaluation. A tool that assists is optional. A tool that replaces a task is defensible.

Measuring by features used, not decisions changed. If your team cannot name a specific decision made faster, a specific handoff eliminated, or a specific hour recovered — the tool is not working. Feature adoption is not business impact. Track the outcome, not the activity.

All three are fixable. None of them are the vendor's fault.

Which of these has cost your team the most in the last 6 months?
